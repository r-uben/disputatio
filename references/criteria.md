# Review criteria

Flag only concrete, verifiable issues. Each comment must point to a specific passage and explain what is wrong with evidence.

## What to flag

1. **Mathematical / formula errors** — wrong formulas, sign errors, missing factors, incorrect derivations
2. **Notation inconsistencies** — symbols used differently than defined, undefined notation
3. **Logical contradictions** — statements that contradict each other or the paper's own framework
4. **Incorrect cross-references** — theorem/equation number mismatches (only when numbered references exist)
5. **Missing proof steps** — compressed or hand-waved steps that skip non-trivial reasoning
6. **Parameter / numerical inconsistencies** — stated values contradict what can be derived elsewhere
7. **Text vs. formal definition mismatch** — prose disagrees with math
8. **Overclaiming** — misleadingly strong or imprecise statements not supported by evidence
9. **Ambiguity that misleads** — could cause a careful reader to reach the wrong conclusion
10. **Sign / direction errors** — wrong sign conventions or reversed inequality directions
11. **Subscript / index errors** — wrong subscript in a specific term

## What NOT to flag

- Writing style, grammar, or readability (unless it creates genuine ambiguity)
- Missing related work or citations
- Suggestions for additional experiments
- Standard conventions in the field (even if unusual in other fields)
- Subjective judgments about significance or novelty
